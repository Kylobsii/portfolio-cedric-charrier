# Study Semester in Montréal

**Where:** _Rubika Montréal_<br>
**When:** _Feb. 2025 – Jun. 2025 (2nd half of 2nd year)_<br>
**Program:** _Exchange semester, working with tech artists_

## Overview

During our **study abroad semester** we got to **work with tech art students** and **meet actors** of the industry there. This semester was also the occasion to **make a decision between continuing to learn about game design, or switching to Programming**:

As I had a bit more ease with coding than most, I ended up doing **a lot of my earlier project as programmer** (there were no programming students before year 3 at Rubika). I **focused more on game design** for these 5 months, trying my hands at **puzzle and narrative design**, alongside all the lessons we had at that time.

## What I worked on & what I learned

I worked **mostly as a designer** for the game [Chasm](projects/Chasm.md), even though **I helped other departments**. You can take a look at the dedicated page for more details.

Alongside focusing on design, I **learned a lot about the industry**. We had classes about about **how to raise funds**, and **calculate the cost of a project**. We learned a lot more about **the "indie" side** of things, whereas we mostly met AAA actors in France. We also **had the chance to visit the Warner Bros Games studio**, which was an awesome opportunity to meet even more developpers.

## Takeaways

The program was in a way **very similar** as we got to learn the same things we would have in France, but also **different in the teaching approach**. During this semester I felt like **I was closer to the industry** than most of the time at school. A lot of our teachers were developpers that would only come and teach us after their day at work, which is a lot different than having full time teachers in France. 

Obviously **living in Montréal was really different** too. Even if it doesn't relate that much to my skills, it was definitely **a learning experience**. I really **grew attached to the place**, and would for sure enjoy coming back and working there full time.
